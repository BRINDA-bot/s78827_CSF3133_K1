About the University

Universiti Malaysia Terengganu (UMT) is a prominent public university located in Kuala Nerus, Terengganu, Malaysia. Officially chartered on 1 February 2007, it began its journey as the Fisheries and Marine Science Centre of Universiti Pertanian Malaysia in 1979.

The institution is widely recognized for its specialized focus on marine science, aquatic resources, and oceanography. It plays a pivotal role in advancing research and education related to marine environments and maritime studies in the region.
